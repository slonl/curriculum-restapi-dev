# Acceptance: Clean shutdown and self-healing Compose startup

Accepted by the maintainer on 2026-10-14 in the connected conversation:

> Accepted

This accepts the evaluated outcome recorded in EVD-20261014-1ZG1H-8 and closes
CYC-20260926-1ZG1H-6. The accepted result includes:

- Compose services use `exec npm start` so normal stop signals reach Node;
- curriculum-store has a 30-second graceful-stop period;
- the curriculum-store pin advances to b3d6faa and SimplyStore 0.11.2;
- after a store SIGKILL leaves its lock behind, the next Compose startup can
  take ownership without manual lock removal when the previous owner's socket
  is proven gone; and
- no automatic restart policy is introduced.

The acceptance retains the recorded safety boundaries. A live or ambiguous
owner, unfinished command, legacy ownership metadata, or multi-host ownership
is not approved for automatic takeover. It also does not claim that the
curriculum-store project's default coverage gate or ESLint configuration is
fixed.

This acceptance closes the cycle but does not authorize a merge by itself.
Prospective integration validation against the current `main` tip remains
required before normal integration.
