# Request: Semantic Continuity Feasibility

## Operationalized Intent

The maintainer needs decision-quality evidence about whether AI-assisted
semantic continuity mapping between independently evolved SLO curriculum dataset
versions is trustworthy and cheap enough to deserve first-class roadmap status
for the maintainer workspace.

## Upstream Understanding

`UND-002` interprets the maintainer's request as a neutral feasibility research
cycle. It explicitly does not assume that AI is an appropriate solution.

## Intended Audience

- The maintainer deciding whether to invest in semantic-continuity assistance.
- Future maintainers who may need to understand why this capability was adopted,
  deferred, narrowed, or rejected.
- Domain experts whose review effort may or may not be reduced by AI assistance.

## Observable Desired Outcomes

### feasibility-decision

The cycle produces a clear positive, negative, or conditional judgment about
whether semantic-continuity assistance should become a first-class
maintainer-workspace roadmap capability.

### evidence-method

The cycle documents a reproducible experiment method, including selected dataset
versions, sample scope, mapping criteria, AI prompts/procedure where material,
and evaluation approach.

### trustworthiness-characterization

The cycle characterizes where AI outputs appear trustworthy, uncertain, or
misleading, especially for renames, hierarchy moves, splits, merges, broadened
or narrowed scope, and redistributed parents.

## Assumptions / Ambiguity

| Claim | Provenance | Evidence / question |
|---|---|---|
| Durable third-party references are a meaningful project concern | explicit | `SRC-002` and project context |
| No ground-truth split/merge migration exists | explicit | `SRC-002` |
| Available dataset versions contain enough comparable material for an experiment | inferred | Must be tested during the cycle |
| AI may or may not be useful | explicit | `SRC-002` |

## Non-Goals

- Do not modify the Open Data maintenance architecture.
- Do not add production import/export or editing features.
- Do not create authoritative `replacedBy` relations for production data.
- Do not claim success from plausible-looking AI output without audit evidence.
- Do not require a complete mapping across all datasets in this cycle.

## Constraints Already Known

| Constraint | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| No known ground truth | `SRC-002` | Evaluation must use auditability, sampled review, consistency checks, and uncertainty characterization rather than accuracy against a gold set |
| Production integration out of scope | `SRC-002` | Keep experiments in research artifacts/scripts/data only |
| Data-format compatibility is high priority | Project context | Do not alter `jsontag`, `od-jsontag`, schemas, or production data |

## Nearest Important Uncertainty

Whether a small, well-chosen sample of independently evolved curriculum material
can produce enough evidence to judge AI-assisted continuity mapping as
promising, not promising, or conditionally promising.

## Risks

| Risk | Horizon | Why it matters | Evidence/action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plausible false mappings | blocker | Would make AI assistance dangerous rather than helpful | Require explanations, uncertainty labels, and maintainer-auditable evidence |
| No representative comparable dataset pair | blocker | The cycle cannot answer the research question without a meaningful sample | First experiment step must select and justify sample pair |
| Evaluation without ground truth overclaims confidence | near-term | Could lead to bad roadmap investment | Separate candidate quality, review effort, and unresolved uncertainty |
| Experiment drifts into production architecture | near-term | Violates cycle scope | Keep integration explicitly out of scope |

## Acceptance Shape

Acceptance should be based on a feasibility report and supporting evidence that
allow the maintainer to decide one of:

- proceed toward a first-class roadmap capability;
- reject AI-assisted semantic continuity mapping for now;
- continue only under narrowed conditions, with specific prerequisites or
  additional evidence needs.
