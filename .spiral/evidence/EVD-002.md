## Failure Cases / Limits

This evidence does not yet identify the exact FO Aardrijkskunde havo/vwo graph
slice to map. FO level IDs match many entities across subjects, so havo/vwo
alone is not a precise sample selector. Further graph-aware extraction is still
needed.

This evidence also does not prove that unlinked entities should receive
continuity links. Missing `replaces` / `replacedBy` may be intentional.

## Evidence Quality

The evidence was produced by parsing JSON files and inspecting fields rather
than by raw text search alone. It can detect whether candidate anchors and
unlinked pools exist, but it cannot by itself assess semantic equivalence.
