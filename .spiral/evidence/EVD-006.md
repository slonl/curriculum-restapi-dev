# Verification Evidence: AI Mapping Pass On Nederlands Literature Sample

## Claim Being Verified

An AI-assisted pass can produce a useful shortlist for the Nederlands havo
literature sample, but the sample does not support unattended continuity-link
creation.

## Result

The useful direction for continuity is older LDK entity to newer FO candidate.
The existing `replaces` fields in this sample remain calibration evidence only;
this evidence does not propose production mutations.

### Calibration Observation

Known intra-dataset continuity anchors are much easier than the intended
cross-dataset task:

- FO `Domein C Literatuur` replaces an older `Domein C Literatuur` with the
  same title.
- FO `Subdomein C1 Literatuur als systeem` replaces an older entity with the
  same title.
- FO eindterms 16-19 replace older concept-eindterms with near-identical
  descriptions.
- LDK `Lezen fictie` replaces an older `Lezen fictie`.

That means the selected sample has good local calibration anchors, but those
anchors are not ground truth for FO-vs-LDK mapping.

## Failure Cases / Limits

This was a single AI-assisted pass by Codex, not an independent comparison
between multiple models or prompts.

The LDK active entities in this slice have sparse text. A better experiment may
need predecessor entities, surrounding `doelniveau` text, or exported human
documentation before a domain expert could quickly accept or reject the
shortlist.

The sample is intentionally small and literature-focused. It cannot establish
that AI assistance works across all Nederlands havo or across other subjects.

## Evidence Quality

The pass is useful because it produced both plausible candidates and explicit
non-links. The most important quality signal is negative: even in a small,
language-rich sample, the best suggestions are mostly `medium` or lower
confidence and require human review. This argues against unattended AI mapping
but supports a possible review-assistance workflow.
