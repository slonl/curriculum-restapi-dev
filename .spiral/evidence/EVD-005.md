Why this sample is useful:

- It is small: one FO domain, two FO subdomains, four FO doelzinnen, one LDK
  vakkern, two LDK subkernen, and four LDK vakinhouden.
- It contains existing `replaces` anchors at the FO domain/subdomain/doelzin
  levels and at the LDK vakkern level.
- It is not trivially identical: FO models literature as system and movement,
  while LDK models fiction reading by reading task and task execution quality.
- It contains likely partial matches and likely gaps, which are important for
  testing whether AI can say "related but not continuity" or "insufficient
  evidence" instead of over-linking.

## Failure Cases / Limits

The LDK sample has sparse descriptions in the extracted active entities, so
some judgments may need deprecated predecessors or additional context before a
domain expert could accept a continuity link.

The sample covers literature only. It does not test Nederlands communication,
writing, speaking, or language-system material.

## Evidence Quality

The extraction follows graph references from the selected FO havo set and the
LDK Nederlands branch. It is strong enough to run the first mapping pass and
weak enough to expose when AI lacks sufficient context.
