# Verification Evidence: Nederlands Havo Is A Better First Sample

## Claim Being Verified

After learning that FO has no Aardrijkskunde yet, Nederlands havo is a better
first semantic-continuity probe sample than a limited Aardrijkskunde-only test.

## Why This Evidence Exists

Cycle 2 needs an experiment that can test semantic continuity across the intended
FO and leerdoelenkaarten dataset family. Aardrijkskunde can still support a
limited calibration test, but it cannot test FO-vs-leerdoelenkaarten continuity
if FO has no Aardrijkskunde content.

## Result

The active sample should switch to Nederlands havo.

FO evidence:

- FO contains 7 active Nederlands sets.
- FO has an explicit active set
  `557e76d0-a417-46fd-aab7-8eedb1d4a86b`,
  "Conceptexamenprogramma Nederlandse taal en literatuur havo".
- That havo set is compact enough for manual audit: 3 domains, 7 subdomains, 19
  doelzinnen, 84 uitwerkingen, and 89 illustraties.
- The havo set has useful continuity anchors: the set has 2 `replaces`, all 3
  domains have `replaces`, all 7 subdomains have `replaces`, all 19 doelzinnen
  have `replaces`, and most uitwerkingen/illustraties have `replaces`.

Leerdoelenkaarten evidence:

- LDK contains active Nederlands vakleergebied
  `9f638551-cd79-439a-a41e-b11e29899164`, which `replaces` two older
  Nederlands vakleergebied entities.
- The LDK Nederlands graph contains 7 `vakkern`, 14 `vaksubkern`, and 65
  `vakinhoud` entities.
- Resolving LDK `doelniveau_id` through `curriculum-basis` gives 50 havo
  `vakinhoud` entities. 30 have `replaces`; 20 have no `replaces`, giving both
  calibration and candidate unlinked material.

Comparison with limited Aardrijkskunde:

- Aardrijkskunde can still support a limited non-FO calibration test, but it no
  longer serves the intended FO-vs-leerdoelenkaarten research question well.
- Nederlands havo offers both sides of the intended comparison and enough
  existing continuity data to audit AI behavior before considering unlinked
  candidates.

## Failure Cases / Limits

Nederlands may be semantically easier or more text-heavy than Aardrijkskunde,
so positive results should not be generalized to every subject without further
sampling.

The first pass still needs a smaller inner slice, because the full Nederlands
havo FO graph is larger than desirable for a first human audit.

## Evidence Quality

The evidence follows explicit subject IDs and graph references where possible,
rather than relying only on text search. It is strong enough to re-plan the
first sample, but not yet an evaluation of AI mapping quality.
