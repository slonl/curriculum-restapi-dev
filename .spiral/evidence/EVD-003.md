# Verification Evidence: Havo Is The First Aardrijkskunde Sample

## Current Status

Superseded by `SRC-005` and `EVD-004`: the maintainer checked that FO has no
Aardrijkskunde yet, so this is no longer the active first sample for Cycle 2.
The evidence remains useful only as a record of why havo was preferred over vwo
inside the now-superseded Aardrijkskunde branch.

## Claim Being Verified

For the first semantic-continuity probe, Aardrijkskunde havo is a more promising
first sample than Aardrijkskunde vwo.

## Why This Evidence Exists

The maintainer had no preference between havo and vwo and asked Codex to pick
the option that looked promising. The choice should be explicit because it
shapes the sample that will be used to judge AI-assisted continuity mapping.

## Result

The first probe should use Aardrijkskunde havo.

Reasons:

- The leerdoelenkaarten Aardrijkskunde graph resolves to the same broad havo and
  vwo coverage at the inspected levels: 9 `vakkern` entities with `replaces`,
  and 25 `vaksubkern` entities of which 19 have `replaces` and 6 do not.
- The active Aardrijkskunde havo exam-program graph is compact enough for a
  manual audit: 6 domains, 12 subdomains, and 26 eindtermen.
- The active Aardrijkskunde vwo exam-program graph is similarly sized: 6
  domains, 10 subdomains, and 28 eindtermen.
- Havo has more local continuity anchors in the active exam-program graph:
  5 havo domains have `replaces`, and 1 inspected havo subdomain has
  `replaces`.
- Vwo has fewer local continuity anchors in the active exam-program graph:
  only 1 inspected vwo domain has `replaces`, and the inspected vwo subdomains
  have no `replaces`.

Additional discovered limitation:

- The local active FO data did not show direct references to the Aardrijkskunde
  shared subject ID or to the Aardrijkskunde havo/vwo exam-program IDs during
  this inspection. This weakens FO as the direct first surface for an
  Aardrijkskunde probe in the local data and should be carried forward as a
  cycle limitation.

## Failure Cases / Limits

This evidence chooses a first havo/vwo level, not the exact inner havo slice.
The next extraction still needs to choose a small graph neighborhood inside
Aardrijkskunde havo.

This evidence also does not prove that the havo sample is representative of the
hardest FO-vs-leerdoelenkaarten semantic drift problem. In fact, the lack of a
direct active FO Aardrijkskunde reference may make this more of a calibration
probe against shared/exam-program continuity than a pure FO-vs-LDK probe.

## Evidence Quality

The evidence compares graph counts and continuity fields after resolving
leerdoelenkaarten `doelniveau_id` references through `curriculum-basis`. It is
strong enough to choose a first sample level, but not strong enough to judge AI
semantic-mapping feasibility.
