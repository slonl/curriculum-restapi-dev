# Understanding: Semantic Continuity Feasibility

## Current Interpretation

The project wants a research cycle, not a production feature cycle, to determine
whether AI-assisted semantic continuity mapping is practically feasible for SLO
curriculum datasets that were independently implemented across time.

The desired evidence is not a definitive continuity graph. The desired evidence
is a trustworthy feasibility assessment: whether AI assistance can reduce the
cost of proposing and validating `replacedBy`-style continuity links enough to
justify making this a first-class maintainer-workspace capability.

The assessment must stay epistemically honest:

- positive, negative, and conditional outcomes are all acceptable;
- lack of ground truth is a central constraint, not an inconvenience to hide;
- false confidence would be worse than a modest or negative result;
- production maintenance architecture changes are out of scope.

## Current Effective Behavior / Evidenced Gap

Historically, continuity relations across independently produced dataset
implementations were often not established because domain-expert work was too
expensive. The current project has no recorded feasibility evidence showing that
AI can produce trustworthy candidate mappings cheaply enough to change that
economics.

Local starting evidence shows `curriculum-store` has versioned dataset snapshots
and history, including tags `v2025.2` and `2026.6`, but the exact candidate
dataset pair and evaluation method still need to be selected during the cycle.

## Provenance Confidence

Evidenced.

## Remaining Uncertainty

- Which available dataset versions best represent independently implemented but
  substantially similar material.
- Whether available metadata and hierarchy are sufficient for useful semantic
  comparison.
- Whether AI can produce candidate mappings with confidence and explanations
  that a maintainer can efficiently audit.
- How to evaluate trustworthiness without a ground-truth mapping.

## Consequence

Create a bounded research request and design an experiment that can produce a
decision-quality feasibility result without modifying production architecture.
